# C3 novelty audit and development protocol

Date frozen for scoped search: 2026-08-15

Status: development hypothesis, not a novelty or performance claim.

## Decision from the prior-art audit

Blind Falsification Jury (BFJ) v2 is retained as a reproducible baseline, but it is not
strong enough to serve as the central novelty of a high-quality paper. The scoped search
found no paper with the exact BFJ implementation, but most of its ingredients already
exist separately:

- CFMAD explicitly forces preset-stance inspection, adds a critic, and uses a third-party
  judge. Its framework describes all possible answers, while its published multi-choice
  experiment and official code use two stances: a three-CoT candidate and one randomly
  selected remaining option.
- P(True) and ownership-bias work evaluates every multiple-choice option independently.
- PRD anonymously ranks candidate answers using multiple peer evaluators.
- Multi-Agent Verification and SpecEM combine multiple verifiers over candidates.
- Response anonymization, self-bias analysis, dynamic weighting, and calibrated judges
  are all established directions.

Accordingly, this project will not claim that anonymity, all-option inspection,
falsification, multi-verifier aggregation, or self-bias mitigation is individually new.

Search families included combinations of `multi-agent`, `ensemble`, `jury`, `judge`,
`verifier`, `candidate`, `each option`, `all options`, `falsification`, `counterexample`,
`certificate`, `anonymous`, `self-bias`, `ownership bias`, `calibration`, `confusion
matrix`, `cross-fit`, `leave-one-out`, `open option`, and `unproposed answer` over arXiv,
ACL Anthology, and OpenReview. This is a scoped search, not proof that no equivalent
method exists.

The later exact searches are consequential: source-calibrated validator TPR/TNR,
commit-first checking, information-asymmetric claim checking, evidence certificates, and
conservative override gates all have direct prior art. None of those ideas is claimed as new.
Cryptographic commit-reveal auditing, learned anti-consensus branch adjudication,
generator-verifier pair analysis, cross-family verifier advantages, and designed information
asymmetry also have direct prior art and are not claimed as contributions.

The v4 search additionally combined `one valid one invalid`, `contrastive reasoning pair`,
`counterfactual trace`, `trace order swap`, `position invariant judge`, `sealed effect`,
`commit reveal`, `paired verifier`, and `cross-model reliability`. It found direct precedents for
valid-to-invalid counterfactual pairs and for order-invariant pairwise judging. It did not find the
exact end-to-end v4 protocol described below. This narrows the candidate contribution to the joint
protocol; it makes any claim based only on contrastive pairs or swapped-order judging indefensible.

Additional exact searches over `effect set`, `option effect`, `option partition`, `sealed
elimination`, `blind reconstruction`, `independent reconstruction`, and `hypergraph` found
POE, verified structured elimination, ORCH, and reconstruction work in other domains. They did
not establish an exact precedent for a sealed signed option-effect set that is independently
reconstructed before reveal and then used as a source-cross-fitted routing signal. This remains
a scoped search result, not proof of novelty.

The v7 search found a close structural precedent that materially narrows the claim. PRePair
explicitly performs pointwise reasoning before pairwise comparison, and independent pointwise
score distributions are also used to compare two responses. Therefore neither isolated judging nor
programmatic recombination is novel. v7 treats these as prior-art controls. Its only remaining
candidate contribution is the full interaction among a post-commit permuted valid/invalid trace
pair, sibling-hidden pointwise audits, target-hidden signed option-effect reconstruction, exact
reveal matching, and source-cross-fitted generator-checker reliability. Failure to outperform the
PRePair-style matched-budget control withdraws that contribution.

The v8 search narrows the claim again. Independent answer commitment before verification is
already a direct control in *More Convincing, Not More Correct*; localized divergence auditing is
the core of AgentAuditor; and Minority Sentinel already learns a conservative majority-override
decision from labeled debate fingerprints. Therefore private precommitment, explicit
countertesting, localized trace auditing, and learned minority intervention are not individually
novel. The only remaining hypothesis is their exact label-firewalled composition with
post-commit trace permutation, sibling-hidden single-trace proof obligations, a sealed signed
trace/effect/option reveal, and source-cross-fitted conditional reliability. This is a scoped search
result, not proof of novelty, and it is withdrawn if a closer implementation is found or if the
matched controls explain the gain.

A later exact-combination search found additional close 2026 work. VOUCH makes both self-side and
cross-side counterfactual answer commitments under a shared numerical intervention. *World Models
in Words* constructs minimally perturbed valid/invalid traces and verifies recomputation and
answer-trace compatibility. RAFS combines targeted counterfactual sensitivity with local trace
validity and answer entailment, while CAGE-CAL calibrates multi-agent dependence against a matched
no-communication graph. CALVER shows that reference-free symbolic verification can rescue a valid
minority trace when task axioms are executable. Consequently, cross-agent commitment,
valid/invalid trace construction, explicit recomputation, counterfactual stability, dependency
calibration, and minority-trace rescue are all excluded from standalone novelty claims. The scoped
search still did not find the exact composition of a post-commit permutation, sibling- and
pair-hidden single-trace proof obligations conditioned on only the checker's frozen private answer,
and delayed sealed trace/effect/option reveal with source-cross-fitted routing. This is only a
search outcome, not proof of novelty; an exact or functionally equivalent precedent withdraws the
claim.

The final integrity search also found *When Latent Agents Lie*, which binds visible specialist
commitments and hidden KV-cache payloads with an HMAC manifest, and reconfirmed that Multi-Agent
Verification already treats verifier count as a test-time scaling dimension. C3 therefore makes no
novelty claim for multiple verifiers, visible commitments, hash-bound manifests, or tamper-evident
artifacts. SAVeR further removes pre-commit auditing, localized violation diagnosis, verifiable
acceptance criteria, and minimal counterfactual repair from the novelty surface. Unlike SAVeR, C3
does not edit and recommit a repaired trajectory; it tests whether information-isolated audits of a
frozen contrastive witness can select among heterogeneous experts. The remaining candidate claim
is therefore narrower than either a general self-auditing or a general commit-before-verification
framework. GLEAN likewise makes step-wise trajectory evidence accumulation and calibrated
correctness estimation prior art, so neither is claimed here. Its narrower hypothesis remains the
equal-call information intervention tested
by the two prompt-level ablations: whether private Stage-0 conditioning plus sibling-trace isolation
improves sealed counterfactual proof auditing after all outputs are frozen.

A same-day final search over 2026 work further rules out several tempting component claims. PEAR
already formalizes permutation-equivariant adaptive routing; SELENE and *Demystifying MAD* cover
selective debate, evidence-weighted aggregation, calibrated confidence, and diversity-aware
initialization; *Hear Both Sides* selects maximally disagreeing messages; and MAD-M2 masks suspected
erroneous context. The Graph-Contract Audit is especially close at the commitment level: it elicits
a causal commitment and tests its consequences in fresh prompts that do not expose that commitment.
MAGNET additionally uses answer-free text-only multi-agent process verification to construct
preference pairs. Therefore C3 claims none of permutation equivariance, adaptive routing, selective
debate, confidence weighting, minority-message retention, generic information masking,
hidden-commitment auditing, or answer-free multi-agent calibration by itself. No searched source
combined those components with C3 v8's two independently frozen private answers, author-created
valid/invalid local pair, post-commit slot permutation, sibling- and seal-hidden single-trace proof
obligations, delayed trace/effect/option reveal, and source-cross-fitted conditional routing. That
is a scoped search result as of the date above, not proof of novelty; failure of either equal-call
prompt intervention withdraws the remaining mechanism claim.

The v9 search starts from the v8 negative rather than relabeling it. Exact searches combined
`sealed diagnostic probe`, `hidden outcome mapping`, `blinded diagnostic subquestion`,
`commit-reveal subquestion`, `answer mapping`, and `candidate-hidden verifier` across arXiv, ACL
Anthology, and OpenReview. CoVe is a direct precedent for planning verification questions from a
draft and answering them independently. MARCH is an even closer precedent for a proposer turning a
response into atomic questions that a checker validates without seeing the response or asserted
answers. SAVeR supplies diagnostic probes and localized countertests, while assessor blinding,
answer-label permutation, question decomposition, and commit-reveal are each established. None of
those components is a v9 contribution. The scoped search did not find a system in which an author
commits a self-contained two-outcome diagnostic and an outcome-to-candidate bijection, deterministic
code renames the outcomes after that commitment, a checker receives neither original task nor
candidates nor mapping, and only code reveals the checker outcome into query-local candidate
evidence after the raw output is written and hashed. That exact composition is the sole candidate
mechanism claim. It is not proof of global novelty and must be withdrawn if a functionally
equivalent method is found or the equal-call mapping-visible and direct-pair controls explain its
gain.

## Version decision after smoke testing

C3 v1 is a retained negative development result. Its bounded Stage-1 smoke parsed 11 of
12 certificates with no truncation, but its Stage-2 smoke parsed only 5 of 8 checks. More
importantly, qualitative inspection showed candidate anchoring: on one question the checker
returned A, B, C, and D respectively as its supposedly independent answer while checking the
certificate for A, B, C, and D. The v1 full run is therefore prohibited.

C3 v2 changes the scientific object rather than merely relaxing the parser. A checker receives
the question, all options, and only the premise/check/failure content of one certificate. It does
not receive the certificate generator, sealed target option, claimed verdict, proposed alternative,
votes, or labels. It must reconstruct the complete set of options eliminated and supported by the
certificate. Only after the output is frozen does deterministic code compare the reconstruction
with the sealed target. This protocol is called target-blind effect reconstruction (TBER).

C3 v2 is also retained as a negative result. Guided decoding produced the requested syntax
without truncation, but only 11 of 28 derived certificates passed semantic parsing in the bounded
two-dataset smoke. Seventeen outputs declared `FALSIFIED` while also declaring `FAILURE: NONE`.
Qualitative inspection additionally found repeated false derivations across many options of the
same MMLU-Pro question. The queue stopped before Stage 2. The parser was not weakened and no v2
full run is authorized.

C3 v3 changes the generation unit from a named candidate to a candidate-neutral, set-valued
witness. It is called sealed effect reconstruction (SER). One model call per question emits an
atomic invariant, a derivation, an applicability boundary, and complete signed sets of options
claimed to be eliminated or supported. The claimed sets, confidence, author, and Stage-0 answer
are sealed from each checker. A checker independently reconstructs the signed sets from only the
question, options, invariant, derivation, and boundary. Reveal and comparison are deterministic.

C3 v3 is a retained negative result after its bounded two-dataset, label-free smoke. All six
generator calls parsed without truncation, but three of six witnesses assigned every option to
the union of eliminated and supported sets, violating the frozen maximum rate of zero. The
historical queue script checked the two sets separately and therefore failed to enforce this
union-coverage gate before starting Stage 2. Stage 2 is retained as a diagnostic: all six calls
parsed and declared the witness valid, but none reconstructed the sealed signed set exactly and
mean signed-set Jaccard was 0.1306. Direct label-free inspection also found dimensional,
relevance, and operator-calculation defects. No v3 full run is authorized, and the parser or gate
will not be weakened post hoc. The hash-bound decision is stored in
`outputs/bench_coe/innovation/cross_examined_certificates/dev_v3_20260815/smoke/negative_smoke_decision.json`.

C3 v4 is a pre-registered development hypothesis named sealed counterfactual parity arbitration
(SCPA). Before any v4 model call, configuration hash
`af9a071171004a2ae33062940d49697ff8c103af3791d589504fe8be73334088` froze the model pool,
prompts, deterministic trace-slot assignment, source data, smoke gates, nested selection grid, and
42-call equal-budget controls. A generator receives only its own already-frozen Stage-0 response and
constructs two minimally different local derivations, exactly one valid, with one sealed signed
option effect. A checker sees neither that private response nor the author, claimed trace, effect,
option, confidence, votes, or labels. It audits both canonical and mirrored trace order. The sealed
trace/effect/option triple is revealed only to deterministic code.

C3 v4 is retained as a negative result after Stage 1. Four of six witnesses parsed (66.7%), below
the frozen 90% threshold; four were non-abstaining, no prompt was truncated, and no witness covered
all options. One failure was a genuine partial seal. The other exposed an implementation defect in
the frozen case-insensitive label-leak expression, which classified lowercase mathematical variables
such as `(x)` as option labels. Direct label-free inspection also found self-contained calculation or
rule defects in several parsed pairs, so correcting that expression alone cannot authorize v4.
Stage 2 and the full run were not started. The hash-bound record is
`outputs/bench_coe/innovation/cross_examined_certificates/dev_v4_20260815/smoke/negative_smoke_decision.json`.

C3 v5 is also retained as a Stage-1 negative. Its corrected label firewall removed the v4
lowercase-variable false positive and its stratified smoke exercised both required trace slots three
times. Nevertheless, only two of six witnesses parsed. Two complete outputs chose the semantically
valid trace but did not obey the externally assigned slot; two others mixed `NONE` and non-`NONE`
seal fields. This motivates v6 commit-then-permute: the author commits freely before deterministic
code applies the hidden trace permutation, and guided generation admits only a complete triple or a
complete low-confidence abstention. v5 Stage 2 and full generation were not run. Its hash-bound

## C3 mechanism

C3 means Cross-Examined Counterexample Certificates.

### Stage 0: independent experts (v8)

Each base expert answers once before any challenge exists. A challenge author may read only its own
frozen Stage-0 response; it cannot read another response, model identity, vote count, popularity,
source score, or label. A checker receives only its own checker-specific frozen Stage-0 response,
explicitly marked as an untrusted precommitment. No model revises its Stage-0 answer, and no model
sees another model's private response.

### Stage 1: commit-then-permute sealed counterfactual challenge (v8)

Each generator rechecks its own private solve and either abstains or returns:

1. one atomic rule, fact, constraint, or calculation;
2. two compact derivations with the same setup;
3. exactly one named first decisive difference;
4. exactly one logically valid trace and one minimally corrupted trace;
5. exactly one signed effect, `ELIMINATES` or `SUPPORTS`, on one option;
6. confidence.

The author first commits to the complete pair and sealed triple. Deterministic code then uses a hash
of seed, question ID, and generator to keep or swap the two displayed trace slots, so the author
cannot adapt its content to the required slot. Option labels are prohibited inside the visible rule
and traces. The post-permutation valid-trace index, signed effect, and option form a sealed triple. A
complete low-confidence abstention is allowed; partial claims and any effect covering more than one
option fail closed.

### Stage 2: sibling-hidden commitment-conditioned proof audit (v8)

Every non-abstaining pair is checked by each other challenge model in two separately frozen calls,
one for each trace. The checker sees the question, options, visible rule, exactly one trace, and only
its own frozen Stage-0 response. It does not see the sibling trace, know that a pair exists, or see
the author, first-difference claim, sealed validity/effect/option, votes, other audits, or label. Each
output must state a concrete countertest, its result, an independent recomputation, relation to the
private precommitment, and either one reconstructed signed option effect or a concrete fatal flaw.
Only after both raw outputs are written does deterministic code combine them. A pair is admitted as
one-valid/one-invalid only when the two independent branches establish that pattern, and its valid
trace/effect/option must exactly match the sealed triple. Parse failures, incomplete proof
obligations, and all other pair patterns fail closed. Both calls are charged to cost.

### Stage 3: conditional reliability aggregation

Only source-development labels may fit the aggregator. For each candidate, observable
features distinguish:

- whether the certificate generator's independent Stage-0 answer equals the candidate;
- whether the checker model's independent Stage-0 answer equals the candidate;
- generator-checker pair identity;
- certificate verdict, confidence, and parse state;
- checker verdicts and cross-check agreement;
- target-blind reconstructed elimination/support, displayed trace, and signed effect;
- concrete countertest result, recomputation, and relation to the checker's precommitment;
- one-valid/one-invalid pair status and exact sealed-triple agreement after delayed combination;
- source-fitted base-expert reliability and query-local vote structure.

A regularized candidate-correctness model is fit in the outer training fold. Its
regularization and intervention margin are selected by leave-one-environment-out folds
strictly inside that training fold. Sparse generator-checker effects shrink toward pooled
effects. Candidate probabilities are normalized only within the current question.

### Stage 4: conservative intervention

The source-fitted best single expert is the fallback. C3 changes that answer only when the
candidate posterior margin clears the source-selected intervention threshold. Because all
options enter Stages 1-3, C3 may rescue an option proposed by no base expert. All
interventions, rescues, harms, certificates, and conditional scores are retained per query.

## What may be novel

The only defensible v8 candidate contribution is the joint mechanism, not its parts:

> private self-conditioned construction followed by deterministic post-commit permutation of a
> minimal valid/invalid reasoning pair; checker-precommitment-conditioned but sibling- and
> pair-hidden single-trace proof obligations; delayed deterministic combination and exact sealed
> trace/effect/option reveal; and source-cross-fitted generator-commitment x checker-commitment x
> generator-checker reliability, coupled to open-option rescue and conservative intervention.

The scoped search found direct prior art for every major component, including contrastive
valid/invalid pairs, counterfactual commitments, recomputation audits, independent pointwise
judging, and source-fitted intervention gates, but no exact match for this end-to-end joint
mechanism. This is not proof of novelty. The contribution must be weakened or withdrawn if a closer
method is found, if v8 does not beat matched-budget controls, or if precommitment, sibling hiding,
sealed reveal, and conditional-reliability ablations do not isolate its gain.

## Required development comparisons

C3 must be compared under aligned questions and model pools against:

- every fixed single expert and the full-development best-single envelope;
- a deployable nested-OOF best-single selector;
- majority, source-accuracy-weighted voting, and source-fitted expert-confusion Bayes;
- cross-model consensus;
- uncalibrated all-option falsification;
- BFJ with no certificate cross-examination;
- direct anonymous multi-verifier scoring;
- AgentAuditor-style localized divergence auditing over the same frozen base responses;
- Beyond-Consensus-style static validator calibration and minority veto;
- Minority-Sentinel-style supervised debate-fingerprint flip gating;
- commit-first candidate-aware verification;
- target-blind reconstruction without learned conditional reliability;
- unsealed set-aware verification using the same witness text and call budget;
- sealed effect reconstruction without reveal-agreement features;
- target-aware cross-examination using the same certificate content;
- EL-DGR-style conservative certificate admissibility where task evidence permits;
- CFMAD-style preset-option critique without a learned calibration layer;
- C3 without generator-answer dependence;
- C3 without checker-answer dependence;
- C3 without generator-checker effects;
- C3 without open-option completion;
- C3 without the conservative intervention gate;
- one-generator and one-checker variants.
- equal-call single-model self-consistency and self-revision baselines.
- original-shape two-candidate and 42-call budget-matched three-candidate PRePair-style controls.

Costs, parsed-output rates, prompt truncation, latency, and calls per question must be
reported. A comparison is not labeled CFMAD unless its actual staged prompts and judge
match the paper closely enough; otherwise it is labeled only as CFMAD-style.

The executable CFMAD-style sidecar follows the released multi-choice experiment's shape:
three independent CoT calls choose the first stance, one option is selected from the
remaining set, each of the two stances receives an abduction, skeptical critique, and
rebuttal, and one judge sees both trajectories. This is exactly 10 calls per backbone per
question. The four prefrozen v8 generator/checker backbones each run that complete pipeline;
their individual 10-call predictions and a deterministic 40-call plurality are both
reported. The adaptation replaces the official code's unseeded `random.choice` with a
SHA-256-derived `seed + question_id + model_id` choice, uses strict parseable outputs, and
targets MMLU-Pro/GPQA rather than the paper's original datasets. It is therefore labeled
`CFMAD-style`, not an official or exact reproduction. Neither base predictions nor C3
certificate/check artifacts enter this control.

This list is executable rather than aspirational. The evaluator writes
`required_comparison_coverage.json`, records the exact artifact method(s) satisfying every row,
and forces `full_required_development_comparison_suite_present` to fail while any row is absent.
All rows that were unresolved at the pre-generation v8 audit now have explicit executable
implementations: artifact-derived AgentAuditor-style and EL-DGR-style readouts, equal-call
candidate-visible and unsealed prompt controls, and the staged CFMAD-style sidecar above.
Implementation is not evidence: each row remains artifact-pending until its bounded smoke and
full prediction artifacts exist, hash-authenticate, and pass its preregistered quality gates. None
is silently aliased to generic voting or post-processing. Consequently, an otherwise positive v8
result cannot authorize a blind test while any generated artifact is absent or fails replay.

## Data and decision boundary

- MMLU-Pro validation and GPQA Diamond epoch-0 unique records are development data.
- All previously observed benchmark sets remain development-only.
- Consumed MuSR test is excluded from all ideation, fitting, selection, and new claims.
- Target labels and correctness may not affect prompts, model pools, fit, hyperparameters,
  seeds, parsing, subsets, stopping, or predictions.
- Pretraining contamination is not claimed to be excluded.
- Negative versions and failed parser/GPU attempts are retained without overwrite.

No new blind test is authorized unless nested OOF C3 improves by at least 5 percentage
points over the deployable best-single reference, beats the best fixed single, is
nonnegative on both development datasets, has an environment-stratified paired 95% CI
strictly above zero, passes the frozen McNemar threshold, and completes all required
ablations. Passing these gates authorizes protocol freezing, not a performance claim.
